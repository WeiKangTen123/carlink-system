SMALL PARTS ON A DAMAGED PANEL
For each panel you found damaged, look at each small part listed for it, in every photo that shows it. These are parts fitted on or in that panel, not the panels beside it. List one only when the sign is on the part itself. Most will be undamaged: that is the expected answer.
- Rear Bumper: Rear Number Plate, both Rear Bumper Reflectors, Parking Sensor, Reversing Camera (when in the bumper), the Tail Lamp above each bumper corner, the Exhaust System tailpipe below it.
- Boot Lid or tailgate: both Boot Lid Lamps, Reversing Camera (in the lid's garnish), Rear Number Plate (when on the lid), Rear Windscreen (tailgate only). With the boot open: Boot Lid Lock, Boot Lid Hinge, Boot Lid Weatherstrip, Boot Lid Inner Trim.
- Rear Quarter Panel: the Tail Lamp on that side, Rear Wheel Arch Trim, Rear Wheel Arch Liner (when the photo shows inside the arch).
- Front Bumper: Front Grille, both Fog Lamps, Front Number Plate, Parking Sensor, the Headlamp above each bumper corner, Engine Undercover (when the lower edge shows).
- Bonnet: Front Grille, both Headlamps where they meet the bonnet, Front Windscreen (its lower edge).
- Front Fender: the Headlamp on that side, Front Wheel Arch Trim, Front Wheel Arch Liner (when the photo shows inside the arch).
- Front Door: Wing Mirror, Front Door Glass. Rear Door: Rear Door Glass.
- Wheel (the rim): the Tyre on that wheel, its sidewall next to the rim damage. Tyre: the Wheel rim at the same spot.
