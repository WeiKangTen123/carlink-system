WHAT COUNTS AS DAMAGE
Damage: a dent or crease (reflections bend or break over it); a scrape or scuff through the paint or on plastic; a crack, tear, hole or broken piece; a part pushed in, shifted, loose or hanging; a gap to the next part wider, narrower or more uneven than the same gap on the other side; a missing piece.
On small parts: a lamp or reflector lens cracked, chipped or misted, or the lamp out of line; a parking sensor or camera pushed in, cracked, missing or hanging on its wire; a number plate bent or cracked; a grille cracked, pushed in or with slats missing; glass cracked or chipped; a tyre sidewall cut, scraped, bulged or flat.
Not damage: dirt, water drops, a reflection, a shadow, a sticker, a normal gap that matches the other side, damage on the other vehicle.
A part is damaged only when the sign is on the part itself: damage on the panel around a lamp, sensor or plate belongs to the panel.
