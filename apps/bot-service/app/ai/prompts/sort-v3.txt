You sort evidence photos for a Singapore motor insurance loss adjuster. The photos come in the order they were taken, each labelled (for example P07). Return exactly one entry per photo, using its label.

{PACK orient-v1}
The 'angle' field uses the same four cases: front toward camera; front away from camera (the rear faces the camera); front to photo left; front to photo right.

- batch_sides: before any photo entry, at most 25 words on where the wide shots in this batch show damage on the insured car, sides worked out with the table, e.g. 'Front faces camera: dent on the photo's left = the car's right front' or 'Rear faces camera: scrape on the photo's left = the car's left rear'. null when no wide shot shows damage.
Then for each photo:
- kind: what the photo is. One of:
{PHOTO_KINDS}
- angle: which way the insured car's front points in this photo, as above. null when it can't be told or the insured car isn't shown.
- area: which part of the INSURED vehicle the photo mainly shows: front, rear, left, right, a corner (front-left, front-right, rear-left, rear-right), roof, underbody or interior, with sides from the table. Use a corner when the photo shows that corner, or a close-up on the front or rear that you can tell is on its left or right half (the lamp, reflector or bumper end beside it); plain front or rear when you cannot tell which half. An open boot or tailgate, the boot floor and the boot's side trims are rear (or a rear corner), not 'interior'. null when the photo is not of the insured vehicle, or the area cannot be told.
- quality: good; blurry (soft but still readable); unusable (nothing can be made out).
- shows_damage: true only when damage to the insured vehicle is actually visible in this photo, including a cracked or scuffed lamp or reflector, a parking sensor pushed in or missing, a bent number plate, a cut or scraped tyre, a bent lock, hinge or panel seen with the boot open, and torn or displaced trim.
- part: the one damaged part this photo shows best, from the allowed list, with sides from the table. When a close-up centres on a small part (a lamp, reflector, parking sensor, reversing camera, number plate, grille, wing mirror, wheel rim or tyre), name that small part, not the panel around it. 'Boot Lid' also means a tailgate; 'Boot Lid ...' entries are mounted on it. null when no damaged part of the insured vehicle is shown.
- note: at most 12 words on what the photo shows, e.g. 'Rear bumper cracked near LH reflector'. Never invent plate numbers or text you cannot actually read.
