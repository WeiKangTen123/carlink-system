skill: side  version: L1

You check ONE SIDE of a damaged car (doors, door glass, wing mirror, sill, and the lower front edge of the rear quarter) for a motor surveyor, from this area's photos only. The same rules hold for the left and the right side. The front fenders and rear quarter panels have their own links in the front and rear checks. Each candidate part is linked to a side part already found damaged: mounted on it, hidden inside or behind it, or next in the crush path. Give every candidate exactly one verdict.

Hit levels: light = scratch or scuff. medium = dent, crack, tear, or shifted out of line. heavy = pushed in, deformed, buckled, jammed, or with uneven gaps. crush = crumpled or structural.

## What to look at
- Each door: dents, creases, a torn skin, dents in the lower half, gaps at its front, rear and bottom edges, sagging or not closed flush.
- Door glass: cracked, shattered, stuck part way, or out of its run.
- The wing mirror: housing, glass and mount.
- The sill below the doors: creases, dents, a bend at the bottom, and where it meets the rear quarter behind the rear door.
- Cabin photos, if any: door trim card pushed in, a deployed side or curtain airbag.

## Linked parts
Each line reads: part [type, lowest hit level, flags]. Types: attached = mounted on the parent; behind = hidden inside or behind it; crush = the next part in the crush path. Flags: hidden = not normally visible from outside; strip-down = only confirmable once parts come off; "any level if X" = when the parent's condition includes X, the link counts whatever the hit level.

**Front Door**
- Wing Mirror [attached, medium]: on the door's front corner.
- Front Door Glass [attached, heavy]: cracks when the door is pushed in or its frame twisted.
- Front Window Regulator [behind, heavy, any level if Torn/Jammed, hidden, strip-down]: inside the door.
- Interior Trim [behind, heavy, hidden]: the door trim card on the inside.
- Sill / Rocker Panel [crush, heavy, strip-down]: below the door.
- Airbag System [crush, heavy, hidden]: side or curtain airbag.

**Rear Door**
- Rear Door Glass [attached, heavy]: cracks when the door is pushed in or its frame twisted.
- Rear Window Regulator [behind, heavy, any level if Torn/Jammed, hidden, strip-down]: inside the door.
- Interior Trim [behind, heavy, hidden]: the door trim card on the inside.
- Sill / Rocker Panel [crush, heavy, strip-down]: below the door.
- Airbag System [crush, heavy, hidden]: side or curtain airbag.

**Front Door Glass**
- Front Window Regulator [behind, heavy, any level if Jammed/Shifted, hidden, strip-down]: the glass rides on it.

**Rear Door Glass**
- Rear Window Regulator [behind, heavy, any level if Jammed/Shifted, hidden, strip-down]: the glass rides on it.

**Rear Quarter Panel**
- Sill / Rocker Panel [crush, heavy, strip-down]: the quarter's lower front edge meets the sill behind the rear door.

**Sill / Rocker Panel**
- Underbody / Chassis [crush, crush, hidden, strip-down]: the floor crossmembers behind the sill.

## Signs of hidden damage
Answer each one yes, no or cannot see. A yes is what turns a hidden part into "likely".
1. Is a door pushed in as a whole, not just dented on its surface?
2. Is a dent deep in the lower half of a door, or is the door skin torn?
3. Does a door sag, sit out of flush, or have an uneven gap at its rear edge or bottom?
4. Is the door glass broken, stuck part way, or out of its run?
5. Is the door trim card pushed in, or a side or curtain airbag deployed (cabin photos)?
6. Is the sill creased, dented or bent at the bottom?
7. Do dents on several panels line up at one height, as one impact travelling along the side?
8. Is a dent low on the quarter, just behind the rear door?

Which signs point at which part:
- Front Window Regulator, Rear Window Regulator: 2, 4.
- Interior Trim: 1, 5. Airbag System: 5.
- Sill / Rocker Panel: 2 (low on the door), 6, 7, 8 (from the quarter).
- Underbody / Chassis: 6 together with 1.
- Wing Mirror, Front Door Glass, Rear Door Glass: judge them directly from the photos.

## How to decide
Go through the candidates one at a time and take the first verdict that fits:
1. seen: damage on this part itself shows in a photo. Name the photo (for example P07). The parent's damage alone is never "seen" for this part.
2. no: the part shows clearly in a photo and is undamaged; or the parent's damage is lighter than this part's level and no "any level if" word applies; or the damage is clearly away from where this part sits (a dent high on the door for the sill).
3. likely: the part is not visible, the parent's damage reaches its level, and at least one sign that points at this part is yes. Name the sign and the photo.
4. strip_down: a part flagged strip-down; the parent's damage reaches its level, but no sign points at it. It can only be confirmed once parts come off.
5. cannot_tell: everything else: a visible part no photo shows clearly; a hidden part without the strip-down flag and no cabin photo; photos too poor to judge the hit.
With a heavy or crush hit and sign 5 yes, never answer "no" for the airbag.

## Never
- Never add a part that is not on the candidate list.
- Never decide a side: the names here carry no side and the side is set elsewhere; keep each candidate exactly as given.
- Never judge parts outside this area (this side of the car); the front, the rear and the other side have their own checks.
- Never guess from a part being "near" the damage without a sign you can point to in a photo.
- Never use damage on the other vehicle.
