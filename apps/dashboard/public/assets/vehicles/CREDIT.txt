Changes made to all five models (the *.v2.glb files here)
---------------------------------------------------------
The dashboard renders the geometry only, as a wireframe, so textures, the
UVs and tangents they used, and vertex colours were removed. Vertices were
welded and quantized and the geometry compressed (EXT_meshopt_compression).
Meshes, node names and triangles are otherwise as published. Reproducible
with apps/dashboard/scripts/optimize-vehicle-models.mjs.
